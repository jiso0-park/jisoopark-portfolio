Photography. Creative Direction. Artistic Creation.

Jisoo Park is a Korean-American fashion and fine-arts photographer based in NYC, Paris, and Seoul. Her unique global experiences from all three cities have shaped her passion in capturing diverse and artistic stories. Each project is a love letter to each city, told through film and portrait photography. By connecting with people worldwide, Jisoo captures the beauty of each city while representing the human stories that make it home.
